## Mutation Gate

The runner was explicitly prepared as reviewed `cargo-mutants 27.1.0`, pinned
and installed with its lockfile after crates.io metadata identified the MIT
package and `sourcefrog/cargo-mutants` upstream.

The first targeted run selected eight `batch_identity` and `claim_values`
mutants. It reported two caught and six unviable because the replacement made
imports or parameters unused under repository `deny(warnings)`. Inspection of
all six logs showed warning-only compile rejection, not survivors. Repeating
the same eight operators with `--cap-lints true` produced eight caught, zero
missed, zero timeout, and zero unviable mutants in 27 seconds. No score is
generalized beyond this targeted set.

A bounded Lua operator kept the close guardian armed after successful explicit
close. The existing focused test failed with two native close calls instead of
one, so the useful operator was caught. No replacement Lua mutation engine or
native/MD mutation score was invented.
