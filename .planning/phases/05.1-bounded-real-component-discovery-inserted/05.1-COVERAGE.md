## Requirement Assignment Decision

Phase 05.1 has no newly assigned milestone requirement ID. The plans use inherited OBS-06
only to name the behavior being strengthened and D-09 to identify its local evidence closure.
The spec-less probe fallback is deliberately skipped because no new requirement ID exists; the
probe's outcome is tracked as human-only evidence rather than a fabricated requirement.

## Implementation and Evidence Checkpoint

Local implementation is conditional and may establish only fake-adapter, static-binding,
serialization, and strict-admission safety behavior. It remains `pending-X4`: native station
availability, call cost, and owner normalization are not established until the human disposable
probe records them. A failed probe retains `facts_unsupported` and routes a separately authorized
revision. It never widens the scan, introduces a cursor, or selects a different native seam
automatically.

## Assumption Delta

The station count/fill call shape and component-local capacity behavior are external X4 runtime
assumptions. Local Lua and Rust tests establish bounded adapter and admission contracts only.
The disposable Creative Custom procedure is the sole path to classify native availability and
cost as observed in X4.
