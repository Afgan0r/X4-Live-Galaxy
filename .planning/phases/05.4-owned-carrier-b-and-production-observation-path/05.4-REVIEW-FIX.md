### T-05.4-MEM: Stale teardown could clear an active replacement producer

**Files modified:** `crates/x4-carrier-native/src/lua_operations.rs`, `crates/x4-carrier-native/src/lua_transport.rs`, `crates/x4-carrier-native/src/lua_producer_operations_tests.rs`, `crates/x4-carrier-native/src/lua_teardown_tests.rs`
**Commit:** `27e9cc3`
**Applied fix:** Reset now validates a present, non-empty reason of at most 64 bytes and uses `HandleRegistry::reset`; close uses `HandleRegistry::close`. Producer and transport teardown occurs only when the registry result proves ownership of the active generation. Stale, malformed, wrong-generation, and already-closed calls retain replacement global state without requesting its transport shutdown. Valid reset preserves the active registry token while clearing its bounded queues and owned producer/transport; valid close deactivates the token and clears the same owned state.
