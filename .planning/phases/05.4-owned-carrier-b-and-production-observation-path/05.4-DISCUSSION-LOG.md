## Distribution and startup

| Approach | Consequence | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portable Windows package; independent X4 and bridge startup | Automatic connection without game-owned process management | Accepted |
| Game/mod-managed bridge startup | Adds external process supervision | Not selected |
| Dedicated joint-launch shortcut or script | Starts bridge with X4 and stops its owned bridge after exit | Deferred |

The owner accepted the baseline, suggested a possible joint startup/shutdown
script, then raised public distribution portability. The agreed conclusion
was to defer joint-launch automation to public installation design. No
automatic process supervisor is required in 05.4.

## Failure recovery

| Approach | Consequence | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Halt observation until manual resumption after send retry exhaustion | Explicit intervention is needed | Not selected |
| Pause failed transfer and check bridge availability | Automatic compatible reconnection starts fresh collection | Accepted |

The owner asked what "infrequent" checks meant. A five-second real-time target
was proposed, conditional on continuing game callbacks and subsequent cost
measurement. The owner approved it. Pause/load have no five-second guarantee.
The target does not authorize unlimited send retries, automatic save resume,
ambiguous continuation or duplicate durable publication.

## Disposable X4 acceptance

The owner explicitly accepted a small real X4 observation flow through durable
publication, reconnection, load/UI reload and SETA. The minimum mod composition
was asked separately and accepted: vanilla, DLC and Live Galaxy with necessary
dependencies. Broader supported-mod compatibility proof remains later work.

The user performs all in-game actions under a written disposable verification
plan. Local/fake evidence does not establish runtime behavior. Exact observation
selection, measurement workload, run duration, error cases and thresholds stay
with research and checked planning under the existing verification contracts.
