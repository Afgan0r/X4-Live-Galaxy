# Phase 05.5: Heavy Faction Ship Conformance - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** Owner-approved discussion; ready for research and checked planning
**Authority:** Final owner confirmation on 2026-09-11, amending ADR-LG-022's
hostile-faction exclusions while preserving the accepted 05.3/05.4 architecture.

<domain>

## Phase Boundary

Prove the shared observation architecture with a deliberately heavy real
faction-ship workload through X4 getters, Lua collection, the owned DLL
producer, Carrier B, Rust assembly, durable publication and compatible decision
snapshots. First prove one heavy ordinary eligible faction; then prove aggregate
coverage of the admitted ordinary factions plus XEN and KHK.

This is an observation conformance phase. It adds no Faction Mind, hostile
strategic architecture, game mutation, reconnaissance system, player report,
custom interface or public-readiness claim. Milestone 0.1 continues to run
Faction Minds only for ZYA and ARG. Station-specific gaps retain Phase 05.6.

</domain>

<decisions>

## Implementation Decisions

### Required observation content

- **D-01:** Core records contain ship identity, owner, type/class and location.
- **D-02:** Cargo observations contain ware types, quantities per ware, capacity
  and occupied volume.
- **D-03:** Crew observations contain counts by role, capacity and qualification
  distribution. Individual crew records are not required.
- **D-04:** Equipment observations contain actually installed engines, shields,
  weapons and turrets, plus ammunition and drones. Deeper per-component state
  is not required by this phase.
- **D-05:** These are required research targets, not claims that a suitable API
  exists or that any specific source returns authoritative complete results.
  Cargo, crew and loadout retain the accepted deterministic bounded-group
  contract, exact core revision and member dependencies, independent capture
  windows, availability and freshness, and eventual group coverage. No global
  simultaneous snapshot or cross-core carry-forward is selected.

### Observation eligibility and Faction Mind participation

- **D-06:** Include all dynamically discovered independent vanilla and DLC
  factions, including small and pirate factions. Sector ownership, a shipyard
  and fleet size are not eligibility prerequisites.
- **D-07:** XEN and KHK are mandatory ship-observation subjects. This supersedes
  both the former ADR-LG-022 exclusions and the intermediate discussion answer
  that still excluded KHK. Neither receives a Faction Mind through this phase.
- **D-08:** The player and mod-added factions remain outside mandatory coverage.
  Service and temporary narrative records require an evidenced exclusion;
  unresolved eligibility blocks closure. Finding no ships does not itself
  establish exclusion or an authoritative empty set.
- **D-09:** Record observation-proof eligibility independently of Faction Mind
  participation. Do not force XEN/KHK into `mind_candidate` solely to collect
  them. The exact representation belongs to research and checked planning.
  Classification must preserve each discovered faction and its disposition.
- **D-10:** Keep the ordered proof: one deliberately heavy ordinary eligible
  faction first, then the full admitted observation set including XEN and KHK.
  Research selects the first faction from measured representative workload.

### Unavailable data and closure

- **D-11:** Source-proven `not_applicable` is acceptable when a characteristic
  does not belong to the object. Zero, empty, absent, stale, inaccessible,
  unknown and unsupported remain distinct states; none may be coerced into
  non-applicability.
- **D-12:** Every applicable field in D-01 through D-04 must be obtained.
  An applicable field that cannot be obtained is an unresolved problem that
  blocks phase closure, including a confirmed limitation of the selected
  source. `unsupported` is diagnostic information, not an acceptance waiver.
  Search for a suitable alternative source; failure to find an API does not
  prove impossibility. Bring concrete residual blockers to the owner through
  the required research/handoff flow rather than silently reducing coverage.
- **D-13:** Preserve the actual strength of source evidence. Transfer completion
  cannot create membership completeness, known-empty semantics or deletion
  authority. Source completeness/identity gaps remain explicit blockers for
  claims that require them. Optional-detail failure never deletes the core
  entity; safe runtime degradation does not mean successful phase completion.

### Disposable acceptance environment

- **D-14:** Use vanilla X4 plus DLC and Live Galaxy. KUDA AI Tweaks and Add More
  Sectors remain later compatibility obligations, not this phase's closure
  gates. Do not reintroduce Carrier A as a runtime dependency or fallback.
- **D-15:** Require normal-time and sustained SETA evidence for independent
  bounds on collection, feedback, assembly, publication, recovery and freshness.
  The one-getter Phase 05.4 result does not establish heavy-workload safety.
- **D-16:** The user starts and operates X4 under a written disposable
  verification plan. Agents may prepare the run and inspect retained evidence;
  they never control X4 or read/modify saves. Exact durations, workload limits,
  lifecycle scenarios and numerical acceptance thresholds are research-owned
  choices based on measurements, followed by owner approval of checked plans.

### Inherited architecture

Reuse the Phase 05.3 contracts and Phase 05.4 production path. Lua owns X4
getters, resumable collection and final callback admission. The owned DLL
copies accepted facts and assembles protocol messages; the external bridge
owns global collection policy, receiver validation, publication and recovery.
Background native work never calls X4/Lua or retains their pointers.

Pump before collection, reserve downstream capacity, bound independent and
aggregate resources, retry immutable bytes without repeating getters, and
preserve distinct local handoff, volatile receipt and durable commit outcomes.
Keep finish-then-validate dependencies, fresh qualification across uncertain
source boundaries and history-only prior revisions after such uncertainty.
Required stale facts block dependent decisions. Automatic save resume remains
unsupported. No new transport algorithm or general research platform is chosen.

### Research-owned choices and approval boundary

Exact APIs, identity/ownership semantics, faction classification evidence,
capability applicability, source consistency, numeric budgets, collection
scheduling, data representation and test decomposition belong to mandatory
research and checked planning. The owner has approved the decisions above,
not a source selection, plan or execution. Return material contradictions to
the owner; ordinary implementation choices preserve these decisions.

## Research Starting Evidence and Unknowns

Phase 05.4 passed its scoped one-getter runtime proof; it did not enumerate
ships or prove faction visibility. Historical source reconnaissance found ship,
cargo, crew and equipment API candidates, but did not establish source
completeness, ownership-transition semantics or aggregate cost.

During this discussion, registered `x4-docs.search` requests with
`snapshot_ids: ["x4-9.00-steam-23660954"]`, `limit: 5` and individual queries
`GetPeople2`, `GetLoadout2` and `faction visibility` each returned zero items,
`coverage: complete`, no conflicts and `next_cursor: null`. The snapshot root
was `blake3:8d30413b740369e8572d6a7647366be338421698596eee84d62fec0415685c79`.
These bounded lookup results identify a gap in that selected index; they prove
neither API absence nor current coverage of other snapshots. No entity was
selected for inspection and no new X4 experiment occurred.

Research must use the shared Docs MCP lookup and complete inspect-pagination
contract, establish current relevant snapshots, and exhaust scoped source
evidence before the prescribed knowledge-gap handoff. Do not use historical
memory or player-scoped examples as arbitrary-faction runtime guarantees.

## Deferred Ideas

SEED-003 owns the accepted public-alpha passive-observation direction: existing
faction objects supply foreign contacts; detection does not automatically
reveal cargo, crew or full equipment; contact loss retains the last observation
and its age without refreshing hidden positions. Exact contact sources,
disclosure policy and aging remain later design/research. Active scout
deployment and intelligence-network construction remain later work.

This phase does not implement that policy, grant omniscience, add hostile minds,
or widen the active ZYA/ARG mind roster. Supported-mod compatibility, station
remainder, reports and full director operational proof retain their owners.
