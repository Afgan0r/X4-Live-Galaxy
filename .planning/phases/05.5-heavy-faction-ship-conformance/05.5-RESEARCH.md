### Locked Decisions

#### Required observation content

- **D-01:** Core records contain ship identity, owner, type/class and location.
- **D-02:** Cargo observations contain ware types, quantities per ware, capacity
  and occupied volume.
- **D-03:** Crew observations contain counts by role, capacity and qualification
  distribution. Individual crew records are not required.
- **D-04:** Equipment observations contain actually installed engines, shields,
  weapons and turrets, plus ammunition and drones. Deeper per-component state
  is not required by this phase.
- **D-05:** These are required research targets, not claims that a suitable API
  exists or that any specific source returns authoritative complete results.
  Cargo, crew and loadout retain the accepted deterministic bounded-group
  contract, exact core revision and member dependencies, independent capture
  windows, availability and freshness, and eventual group coverage. No global
  simultaneous snapshot or cross-core carry-forward is selected.

#### Observation eligibility and Faction Mind participation

- **D-06:** Include all dynamically discovered independent vanilla and DLC
  factions, including small and pirate factions. Sector ownership, a shipyard
  and fleet size are not eligibility prerequisites.
- **D-07:** XEN and KHK are mandatory ship-observation subjects. This supersedes
  both the former ADR-LG-022 exclusions and the intermediate discussion answer
  that still excluded KHK. Neither receives a Faction Mind through this phase.
- **D-08:** The player and mod-added factions remain outside mandatory coverage.
  Service and temporary narrative records require an evidenced exclusion;
  unresolved eligibility blocks closure. Finding no ships does not itself
  establish exclusion or an authoritative empty set.
- **D-09:** Record observation-proof eligibility independently of Faction Mind
  participation. Do not force XEN/KHK into `mind_candidate` solely to collect
  them. The exact representation belongs to research and checked planning.
  Classification must preserve each discovered faction and its disposition.
- **D-10:** Keep the ordered proof: one deliberately heavy ordinary eligible
  faction first, then the full admitted observation set including XEN and KHK.
  Research selects the first faction from measured representative workload.

#### Unavailable data and closure

- **D-11:** Source-proven `not_applicable` is acceptable when a characteristic
  does not belong to the object. Zero, empty, absent, stale, inaccessible,
  unknown and unsupported remain distinct states; none may be coerced into
  non-applicability.
- **D-12:** Every applicable field in D-01 through D-04 must be obtained.
  An applicable field that cannot be obtained is an unresolved problem that
  blocks phase closure, including a confirmed limitation of the selected
  source. `unsupported` is diagnostic information, not an acceptance waiver.
  Search for a suitable alternative source; failure to find an API does not
  prove impossibility. Bring concrete residual blockers to the owner through
  the required research/handoff flow rather than silently reducing coverage.
- **D-13:** Preserve the actual strength of source evidence. Transfer completion
  cannot create membership completeness, known-empty semantics or deletion
  authority. Source completeness/identity gaps remain explicit blockers for
  claims that require them. Optional-detail failure never deletes the core
  entity; safe runtime degradation does not mean successful phase completion.

#### Disposable acceptance environment

- **D-14:** Use vanilla X4 plus DLC and Live Galaxy. KUDA AI Tweaks and Add More
  Sectors remain later compatibility obligations, not this phase's closure
  gates. Do not reintroduce Carrier A as a runtime dependency or fallback.
- **D-15:** Require normal-time and sustained SETA evidence for independent
  bounds on collection, feedback, assembly, publication, recovery and freshness.
  The one-getter Phase 05.4 result does not establish heavy-workload safety.
- **D-16:** The user starts and operates X4 under a written disposable
  verification plan. Agents may prepare the run and inspect retained evidence;
  they never control X4 or read/modify saves. Exact durations, workload limits,
  lifecycle scenarios and numerical acceptance thresholds are research-owned
  choices based on measurements, followed by owner approval of checked plans.

#### Inherited architecture

## Summary

The generic architecture is ready to receive this workload, but planning the X4 collector is blocked. The current repository already separates core identity from deterministic bounded detail groups, pumps pending transport before admitting collection, applies permits/heavy permits/debt, validates and durably publishes observations, and keeps recipient visibility separate from collection. [VERIFIED: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:449-497`; `crates/observation-ingest/src/scheduler.rs:116-183`; `crates/observation-ingest/src/scheduler_budget.rs:54-135`; `crates/strategic-state/src/derive.rs:101-114`]

The registered X4 documentation snapshot returned zero entities for every scoped search. That verifies a corpus gap only. It supplies no inspectable signature, ownership rule, completeness guarantee, applicability rule, or lifecycle behavior for faction discovery, arbitrary-owner ship enumeration, cargo, crew, or loadout. The installed X4 distribution exposes the game primarily as catalogs in this environment, and no catalog extraction tool was available; the relevant sibling repository supplies player-scoped call-site precedents but cannot promote them into arbitrary-faction guarantees. [VERIFIED: x4-docs search responses recorded in “X4 Documentation Research Ledger”; VERIFIED: `docs/architecture-verification.md:170-181`; VERIFIED: sibling `live-mcp/extensions/x4_live_mcp/ui/x4_live_mcp.lua:708-719,1200-1213,1453-1484,1629-1644,1771-1782,3185-3195,3880-3891`]

**Primary recommendation:** do not create implementation tasks that select X4 APIs yet. First enrich or otherwise obtain an authoritative, legally usable X4 9.00 source snapshot, rerun the exact lookup/inspect protocol, and convert unresolved semantics into narrow disposable probes. Planning may proceed only for source-independent scaffolding if the plan explicitly gates all source-dependent work and closure on that evidence.

## Architecture Patterns

### System Architecture Diagram

```text
X4 pulse/event
  -> pump pending Carrier B bytes and control feedback
  -> capacity available?
       no  -> visible backpressure; retain continuation
       yes -> admit one bounded Lua collection step
                -> faction census / core ship index
                -> deterministic cargo | crew | loadout group
                -> source/applicability/coverage disposition
  -> owned DLL copies facts and assembles immutable messages
  -> named-pipe transport and receiver validation
  -> keyed Rust candidate assembly
       -> exact core revision still current?
            no  -> reject dependent group; recollect later
            yes -> atomic durable publication
  -> independent durable readback
  -> compatible decision snapshot with separate recipient policy
```

The ordering and dependency behavior follow the accepted architecture. [VERIFIED: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:449-497`; `.planning/phases/05.5-heavy-faction-ship-conformance/05.5-CONTEXT.md:99-113`]

### Recommended Project Structure

```text
extensions/live_galaxy/lua/              # X4 getters, resumable collectors, admission
extensions/live_galaxy/tests/            # pure-Lua and fake-adapter contracts
crates/observation-domain/               # typed core/detail values and evidence axes
crates/observation-ingest/               # limits, scheduler, keyed candidate assembly
crates/observation-application/          # dependency validation and publication
crates/observation-persistence/          # atomic durable current/history/receipt state
crates/x4-carrier-native/                 # owned producer and transport assembly
crates/x4-bridge/                         # production integration and recovery
tests/x4-disposable/                      # human-run X4 procedure and retained evidence index
```

This is a mapping of existing repository responsibility, not a mandate to create every listed file. [VERIFIED: repository tree and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`]

### Pattern 1: Core First, Deterministic Dependent Groups

Core contains exactly the phase-required identity, owner, type/class, and location. Cargo, crew, and loadout are separate deterministic, versioned, bounded groups carrying exact member identities, exact source-core revision, capture window, coverage, and freshness. A core change may finish the running group but invalidates it before publication if its frozen dependency is no longer current. [VERIFIED: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:463-497`; `docs/architecture-decisions.md:397-413`]

### Pattern 2: Pump Before Collection

The current Rust scheduler stages at most one pending byte vector, reserves terminal capacity, pumps pending bulk first, and only then selects a new admission. [VERIFIED: `crates/observation-ingest/src/scheduler.rs:116-166`]

The current Lua scheduler likewise calls `progress` and `poll_control` before `begin_evidence` and capture. [VERIFIED: `extensions/live_galaxy/lua/live_galaxy_scheduler.lua:27-52`]

### Pattern 3: Measured Heavy-Work Admission

The Rust budget admits a heavy intent only when ordinary permits, zero debt, in-flight capacity, step bounds, and a heavy permit all allow it. Completion restores the heavy permit and converts work above declaration into bounded debt or collector rejection. [VERIFIED: `crates/observation-ingest/src/scheduler_budget.rs:54-135`]

The Phase 05.4 Lua constants quote `max_records = 1`, `max_work = 1`, `max_batches = 1`, and `max_age_millis = 5000`; they are one-getter proof values, not valid heavy-workload defaults. [VERIFIED: `extensions/live_galaxy/lua/live_galaxy_carrier.lua:9-22`; VERIFIED: `docs/architecture-verification.md:359-369`]

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **API by precedent:** a working player-scoped call site does not prove arbitrary-faction support, complete membership, stable identity, or field applicability.
- **Empty means complete:** zero ships, empty cargo, or absent equipment cannot become `known_empty` without source completeness evidence.
- **Transport completion upgrades source quality:** successful delivery says nothing about source membership or deletion authority.
- **Global snapshot fiction:** never attach all detail groups to an invented simultaneous faction-wide capture.
- **Detail failure deletes core:** preserve core and publish explicit unknown/stale/unsupported detail state.
- **One-getter constants become production limits:** heavy limits must be derived from Phase 05.5 measurements.
- **Collection implies visibility:** do not expose foreign detail to ZYA/ARG or change `mind_candidate` to make collection easier.

Each anti-pattern is prohibited by the accepted context or verification contract. [VERIFIED: `.planning/phases/05.5-heavy-faction-ship-conformance/05.5-CONTEXT.md:39-83,99-122`; `docs/architecture-verification.md:195-201,433-478`]

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Mistaking corpus absence for API absence

**What goes wrong:** the plan declares an API unavailable because Docs MCP returned zero entities.

**Why it happens:** `coverage: complete` applies to the selected indexed corpus/query, not the game’s total API surface.

**How to avoid:** enrich the snapshot or obtain authoritative source, then inspect selected entities through all cursors; probe only remaining semantic gaps.

**Warning signs:** implementation tasks cite memory or a player-only example instead of exact snapshot/entity provenance. [VERIFIED: `docs/architecture-verification.md:170-181`]

### Pitfall 2: Count/fill races become silent truncation

**What goes wrong:** population grows or shrinks between count and fill, producing a partial set, duplicate, or out-of-bounds assumption.

**How to avoid:** source-specific tests must define allocation/fill behavior, bounded retry, ordering/canonicalization, and explicit partial/unknown coverage.

**Warning signs:** equal pre/post counts or digests are used as an ABA-proof completeness certificate. [VERIFIED: `docs/architecture-verification.md:183-201`]

### Pitfall 3: Optional detail weakens mandatory acceptance

**What goes wrong:** a failed cargo/crew/loadout source is safely represented as unknown and then treated as phase success.

**How to avoid:** preserve core at runtime, but separately block closure whenever an agreed applicable field is unavailable.

**Warning signs:** `unsupported` appears in acceptance totals as passed. [VERIFIED: `.planning/phases/05.5-heavy-faction-ship-conformance/05.5-CONTEXT.md:66-83`]

### Pitfall 4: SETA accelerates demand but not real-time service

**What goes wrong:** game-time freshness demand rises while real-time permit refill, transport, and Rust commit capacity do not.

**How to avoid:** use game time for urgency, real time for admission, reserve core service, degrade detail first, and expose stale-block decisions.

**Warning signs:** queue/backlog slope stays positive or detail retries starve core work. [VERIFIED: `docs/architecture-verification.md:149-153,391-415`]

### Pitfall 5: Reconnect appears idempotent but repeats getters

**What goes wrong:** transport retry triggers a fresh X4 read, changing content under the same logical revision.

**How to avoid:** freeze the accepted record/message and retry immutable bytes; after uncertain source boundaries require fresh qualification.

**Warning signs:** getter-call counters increase during transport-only retry. [VERIFIED: `.planning/phases/05.5-heavy-faction-ship-conformance/05.5-CONTEXT.md:107-113`]

### Pitfall 6: Collection leaks into recipient policy

**What goes wrong:** XEN/KHK inclusion or foreign ship collection becomes a `mind_candidate` or universal visibility change.

**How to avoid:** keep observation eligibility and mind participation separate; verify current decision snapshots remain compatible without adding reconnaissance.

**Warning signs:** changes to mind roster, contact aging, disclosure, reports, or active scouting appear in Phase 05.5. [VERIFIED: `.planning/phases/05.5-heavy-faction-ship-conformance/05.5-CONTEXT.md:46-64,224-237`]
