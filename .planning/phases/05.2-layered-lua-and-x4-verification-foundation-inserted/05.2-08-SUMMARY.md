## Decisions Made

- The generated Lua entrypoint is metadata-only. Local candidate execution belongs to the explicit developer dispatcher, not to X4 UI load or a direct `ffi.C` path.
- Candidate results remain `authenticated-local-contract`; they cannot become `observed-in-X4`, and producer attestation cannot make them X4 evidence.
- A missing owner root/certificate/key is a valid local-readiness outcome with `PRODUCER_ATTESTATION_UNCONFIGURED`, never a retainable success.
- Producer certificate and key paths are not CLI inputs. The root is part of the generated digest graph, the certificate location is fixed under the owner's local application data, and the CNG public key must match the root-signed delegated SPKI.
