## Decisions Made

- Timeout is an externally supplied deterministic stage marker, not wall-clock sampling inside the pure harness.
- Digesting is injected and verified against the exact canonical payload; missing, erroneous, malformed, or mismatched digests fail closed.
- Raw exception text is never serialized. Evidence exposes only closed execution, contract, effect, timeout, mismatch, and digest reason codes.
