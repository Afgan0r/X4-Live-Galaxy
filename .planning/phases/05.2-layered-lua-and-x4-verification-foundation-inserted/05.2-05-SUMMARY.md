## Decisions Made

- Private retention and public admission are separate trust boundaries. The retention helper produces a private locator and a separately validated sanitized object; the admission process reads only sanitized ledgers and committed public contracts.
- The committed pending ledger anchors immutable build and source identities. A later completed ledger may add evidence, run, and locator digests but cannot replace those anchors.
- `effect_verdict = pass` is insufficient by itself: the sanitized actual effect ID must equal the committed expected effect ID.
