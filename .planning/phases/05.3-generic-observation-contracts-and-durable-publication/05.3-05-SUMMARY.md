## Accomplishments

- Ranked core work before detail, then urgency descending and stable FIFO, without letting game-time urgency change any resource allowance.
- Pumped immutable pending bytes before collection while keeping the configured terminal reserve unavailable to bulk traffic.
- Enforced exact step work, finite heavy permits, identity-bound completion, queue capacity, burst exhaustion, and bounded overrun debt.
- Repaid debt before refill by at most one step-work allowance per pulse, with no frozen-clock progress or per-pulse missed-interval loop.

## Admission and Debt State Machine

1. `enqueue` rejects declared work above the injected step allowance before occupying queue capacity.
2. A pulse first refills from injected monotonic real time, then pumps one whole pending immutable message if it fits after terminal reserve.
3. With no pending message, the stable queue selects at most one eligible intent. Admission consumes one real-time permit, exact declared work, and one heavy permit when applicable.
4. `complete` matches the exact admitted identity. Unknown and duplicate completion are no-ops; a matching heavy completion releases exactly one heavy permit.
5. Positive actual-minus-declared work becomes visible debt. Debt caps at the injected maximum and returns `CollectorRejected` when completion would exceed it.
6. Later pulses repay debt before adding permits. One pulse accounts for at most one step-work allowance of debt or one permit and never loops over missed intervals.

## Boundary Matrix

| Dimension | Exact boundary | One-over or blocked boundary | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stable queue | Four accepted intents | Fifth rejected | Accepted entries remain unchanged |
| Step work | Four work units admitted | Five rejected at enqueue | No permit or queue slot consumed |
| Heavy permits | One heavy in flight | Second heavy waits | Exact completion releases once |
| Burst | Two frozen-clock admissions | Third pulse rejects admission | Queue and debt remain intact |
| Terminal reserve | Six bulk bytes with two reserved | One byte short retains all six | No partial pump or collection |
| Overrun debt | Debt reaches eight | Nine reports collector rejection | Debt stays capped without wrapping |
| Frozen clock | Zero elapsed quanta | Repeated pulse | No debt or permit progress |
| Coarse clock | Available quanta exist | One pulse cannot loop | At most four debt units or one permit accounted |
