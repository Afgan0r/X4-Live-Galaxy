## Security Controls

- **Spoofing:** distinct non-empty identities and non-zero epoch/revision/version types reject substitution and empty or zero input.
- **Tampering:** raw message size is checked before Serde; tagged variant fields reject unknown or cross-variant content; exact identity with changed bytes conflicts.
- **Denial of service:** one immutable slot, whole-message limits, finite non-zero scheduling limits, saturating refill, and capacity gating bound work.
- **Information disclosure:** lifecycle errors carry typed reasons and retain no rejected raw payload.
- **Authority expansion:** `ObservationCarrierFacade` admits only connection state, complete-message send, and bounded control polling.
