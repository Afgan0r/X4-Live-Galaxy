## Security Findings

- Tampering is bounded by ordered SHA-256 batch-manifest and canonical-content digests plus exact count, length, identity, and contract-version checks.
- Replay returns the prior received disposition only for identical bytes; equal identities with changed bytes reject and release the affected candidate.
- Resource exhaustion is bounded independently by per-candidate and aggregate bytes, decoded estimate, records, batches, work, age, inactivity, and candidate count.
- Stale-scope confusion fails closed through source uncertainty, dependency/current-pointer checks, and history-only retirement.
- Diagnostics retain bounded typed reasons and identities, never raw candidate payloads.

No new security-relevant surface was introduced outside the plan threat model.
